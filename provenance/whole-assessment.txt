1. ACTUAL CATEGORICAL MODEL, INCLUDING CHARGED CAPS

The pointed diagonal factor is valid. The neutral equivariant invertibles form an abelian extension of the fixed diagonal F_p by the electric C_3; its unique p-Sylow has metric q^3, nondegenerate since p!=3. Its centralizer therefore supplies the asserted product decomposition. De-equivariantization by the electric subgroup preserves the pointed factor; ranks and homogeneous dimensions give one reduced defect T_a of dimension p in each nontrivial grade. Frobenius reciprocity and dimensions give T_a T_{-a}=sum_{v in W}v and T_a T_a=p T_{2a}. Thus all listed outputs and all raw multiplicity dimensions have categorical, rather than just homological, meanings.

Barmeier–Schweigert's marked-cover construction includes lifted diffeomorphisms, sewing, boundary marked lifts, and the monoidal compositor of the group action, not merely the vector spaces of covers. Their Theorem 21 and equations (18)–(21) identify the tensor-induced action; their complete Theorem 26 construction then supplies the multiplicity-space action used here. Deshpande–Mukhopadhyay Corollary 2.11 upgrades the weak fusion category to a rigid fusion category. These are prior inputs. The grade-zero source is the original modular category, so its rigidity hypothesis is satisfied. The positive spherical convention is consistent with integrality and the odd invertible group of order 3p; no nontrivial spherical sign character remains.

The Wilson argument does establish the necessary stronger intertwiner. In the strict pointed model, meridian characters and longitude shifts have commutator psi(omega). Normal ordering by one-half intersection gives W_h W_k=psi(omega(h,k)/2)W_{h+k}. Fusion adds colors, contractible loops have value one, and the peripheral cap calculation is precisely double braiding psi(sum r_i b_i). These skein relations identify the homological quotient, including its two-dimensional peripheral radical. For fixed b, specialization of that radical leaves the full Weyl basis of End(C[F_p^g]); hence the raw representation is the irreducible Heisenberg module, not an unspecified module with the same dimension.

Ribbon insertion is natural under lifted mapping cylinders: surface framing and the half-intersection normalization are carried together. Thus covariance is the asserted action on punctured homology, with no discarded peripheral character. The difference between an actual crossing and a finite Weil/affine-Weil implementer is a scalar. The nonzero ribbon trace fixes that scalar below. Boundary Dehn twists can still act as scalars; they have not been inferred from closed homology alone. For a nonzero orbit output the three boundary-label summands are cyclically permuted. Braids preserve the individually marked outgoing sheets and commute with equivariance. Invariants therefore identify with one charged summand carrying exactly the action just constructed. This is the decisive categorical bridge missing from a bare cover-monodromy calculation.
